About Us

We turn words into geometry.

Friday is a text-to-CAD AI. You describe a part, we work out the operations, check them, build the model and hand you a file.

Accuracy is everything.

A CAD model that looks right but has the wrong dimensions is worse than none. So the language model never draws geometry directly: it produces a structured plan, and that plan is validated before anything is built.

Under the hood, a pipeline clarifies your request, generates the operations, validates the schema and geometry, builds the model, and exports it.
